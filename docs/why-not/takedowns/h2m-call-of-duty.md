# H2M — Call of Duty (Activision, 2024)

**What it was**: a free, volunteer-built mod recreating *Call of Duty: Modern
Warfare 2* (2009) multiplayer inside *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare Remastered*
(2016), whose official release shipped the campaign only.

**Why it matters**: H2M is the cleanest counter-example to "you're safe if you
own the game and ship nothing leaked."

- It **required a legitimate retail copy** of Modern Warfare Remastered to run.
- It **shipped no leaked build or leaked code** — it was a client-side mod on top
  of a legally purchased game.

**Timeline**:

- Planned launch **2024-08-16**.
- **~1 day before launch**, Activision sent a cease and desist. The team complied
  and shut the project down permanently.
- Hours later the client and server files leaked to Discord; hundreds of players
  ran them on community-hosted servers.

**Stated trigger**: project lead Watchful Wolf said (on YouTube) that Activision's
concern was H2M's popularity interfering with *Black Ops 6* sales and marketing —
not leaked assets. There was no asset claim to make.

**Lesson**: high-profile fan work can take a **pre-launch** C&D regardless of
legality, when it competes for the same audience as an imminent commercial
release. Shutting down does not stop the files from circulating.

*Sources: PC Gamer, IGN, Rock Paper Shotgun, Insider Gaming (2024-08-15..20).*
