***The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered*** is a 2025 [action role-playing game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_role-playing_game) co-developed by [Virtuos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtuos) and [Bethesda Game Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethesda_Game_Studios) and published by [Bethesda Softworks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethesda_Softworks). It is a [remaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remaster) of 2006's *[The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_IV:_Oblivion)* and includes a full graphics overhaul using [Unreal Engine 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unreal_Engine_5) and various other improvements, from sprinting to redesigned menus. *Oblivion Remastered* includes all of the original game's [downloadable content](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downloadable_content), including the expansion packs *[Knights of the Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Nine)* and *[Shivering Isles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivering_Isles)*. The game was announced and released digitally for [PlayStation 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5), [Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows), and [Xbox Series X/S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_Series_X/S) on April 22, 2025, to generally positive reviews from critics. It was released physically for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S on October 13. A [Nintendo Switch 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Switch_2) version was released on August 11, 2026.

## Gameplay

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Elder_Scrolls_IV:_Oblivion_Remastered&action=edit&section=1)]
See also: [*The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion* § Gameplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_IV:_Oblivion#Gameplay)
*The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered* retains the [open world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_world) structure and core gameplay mechanics of the original, including real-time combat, character leveling, skill-based progression, and questlines tied to multiple factions.[[1]](#cite_note-1) The remaster includes a full graphical overhaul with modern rendering techniques, [ray tracing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_tracing_(graphics)), and updated textures and models. [Non-playable character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-playable_character) behavior has been revised using improved AI, and all animations have been updated. The [user interface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User_interface) and control schemes have also been redesigned for compatibility with current-generation hardware. Audio enhancements include re-recorded ambient sounds, environmental effects, and limited redone voice work.[[2]](#cite_note-2)[[3]](#cite_note-3) *[Knights of the Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_of_the_Nine)* and *[Shivering Isles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivering_Isles)*, which are paid [expansion packs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expansion_pack) of the original game, are included in the remaster.[[4]](#cite_note-4)

## Plot

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Elder_Scrolls_IV:_Oblivion_Remastered&action=edit&section=2)]
Main article: [*The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion* § Plot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_IV:_Oblivion#Plot)

*The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered* is set in the province of Cyrodiil, and the player takes the role of the Hero of Kvatch, who is drawn into a conflict involving the Daedric realm of Oblivion after the assassination of Emperor Uriel Septim VII.[[5]](#cite_note-5)

## Development and release

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Elder_Scrolls_IV:_Oblivion_Remastered&action=edit&section=3)]
*The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered* was co-developed by [Virtuos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtuos)' Paris studio and [Bethesda Game Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethesda_Game_Studios). Both studios began development of the remaster in 2021. The developers used [Unreal Engine 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unreal_Engine_5) to enhance the visuals, while relying on the [Gamebryo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamebryo) engine for the core to enhance elements like physics and combat.[[6]](#cite_note-6) Initial indications of the remaster emerged in September 2023, when internal Microsoft documents (dated 2020) disclosed during the *[Federal Trade Commission v. Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Trade_Commission_v._Microsoft)* case referenced, among other unannounced titles, an "*Oblivion* Remaster" slated for release in the 2022 fiscal year.[[7]](#cite_note-7)

Further speculation arose in April 2025 following the discovery of in-game images and promotional materials on Virtuos' website, which were subsequently shared online. The images, found through an accessible [WordPress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WordPress) file repository, showed recognizable themes such as the player's first view of the outside world upon leaving the tutorial quest, the Imperial City and its surroundings, and several other highlights.[[8]](#cite_note-8)[[9]](#cite_note-9) On April 21, 2025, Bethesda confirmed the remaster's existence by announcing a livestream scheduled for the following day,[[10]](#cite_note-10)[[11]](#cite_note-11) when the game was released for [Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows) through [Steam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_(service)), [PlayStation 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_5), and [Xbox Series X/S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_Series_X/S), in addition to day-one availability on the [Xbox Game Pass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_Game_Pass).[[12]](#cite_note-12) The live announcement on [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube) was watched by more than 600,000 people.[[13]](#cite_note-13) On July 14, The Game Collection (an online video game retailer) announced that physical editions for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X would release on October 13.[[14]](#cite_note-14)[[15]](#cite_note-15) The remaster is set to release on [Nintendo Switch 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Switch_2) on August 11, 2026.[[16]](#cite_note-16) The physical Switch 2 version of the title contains the entire game on a [cartridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Game_Card),[[17]](#cite_note-17) contrary to Bethesda's comments[[18]](#cite_note-18) - which stated that the game would contain a download code only in the box as with the Switch 2 physical releases for the *Anniversary Editions* of *[Fallout 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallout_4)* and *[The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_V:_Skyrim)*.[[19]](#cite_note-19)

On April 22, 2025, Bethesda stated that the remaster will not officially support [modding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_modding). This was seen as unusual by various news outlets due to the company being generally supportive of the practice, adding extensive mod support to most of their titles, including their previous major release *[Starfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starfield_(video_game))*.[[20]](#cite_note-20)[[21]](#cite_note-21) Despite the lack of official mod support, [user-created](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User-generated_content) mods began emerging rapidly after the game's release.[[22]](#cite_note-22)[[23]](#cite_note-23)
