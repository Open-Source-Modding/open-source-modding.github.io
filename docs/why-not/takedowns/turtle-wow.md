# Turtle WoW — Blizzard Lawsuit (2025–2026)

**What it was**: a private World of Warcraft server running since 2018, free to
play but monetized through donations and a shop. It also shipped modded clients
and an Unreal Engine "remaster", **Turtle WoW 2.0**.

**Case**: `Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. et al. v. Turtle Wow et al.`,
**2:25-cv-08194**, Central District of California.

- Filed **2025-08-29**; terminated **2026-04-15**.
- Judge **Stephen V. Wilson** ruled for Blizzard on all seven causes of action.
- A **stipulated permanent injunction** against AFKCraft Ltd., Josiah Zimmer
  (later Jesse Lautenbach) bars private/emulated servers, modded clients, the
  Turtle WoW 2.0 remaster, and soliciting donations.

**Outcome**: shutdown announced 2026-04-18 — servers closed **2026-05-14**, social
channels due to close **2026-10-16**.

**Stated trigger**: Blizzard alleged the server cannibalized live WoW player
counts. This is the case that escalated past a letter — the one to cite when
someone says cease and desists are "just letters."

**Lesson**: running someone else's live-service game as an emulated server is the
riskiest category here, and taking money for it makes the case worse. This is the
sharply-litigated end of the spectrum.
