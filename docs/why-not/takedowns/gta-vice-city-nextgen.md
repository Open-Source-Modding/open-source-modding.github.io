# GTA Vice City Nextgen Edition (Take-Two / ESA, 2025)

**What it was**: a port of *GTA: Vice City* into the RAGE engine of *GTA IV*, made
by the Russian team **Revolution Team**. It initially required a legitimate GTA IV
copy to run.

**Timeline**:

- **~2 days before** the planned **2025-01-25** release, **Take-Two terminated the
  team's YouTube channel** via third-party copyright claims.
- The team rebuilt the release as **standalone** (no GTA IV required) and shipped
  on schedule anyway.
- In **February 2025**, an **ESA** complaint (delivered via VK) forced them to
  remove the download links and end support.

**Context**: the project reused Vice City assets and music, and it landed while the
*GTA: The Trilogy — The Definitive Edition* was the paid alternative. Former
Rockstar developer **Obbe Vermeij** was blunt: "This is what companies are
supposed to do… directly competes with the Definitive Edition."

**Lesson**: platform enforcement (channel termination by copyright claim) can
arrive before any legal letter, and rebuilding to remove the dependency does not
save the project — the second wave came from the industry body, not the publisher.

*Sources: IGN, Eurogamer, GTA BOOM, GamesRadar, Sportskeeda, RockstarINTEL,
Substack.*
