# The Last of Us Part 2 Multiplayer Mod (Sony, 2026)

**What it was**: a Factions-style 4v4 PC multiplayer mod for *The Last of Us Part
2*, built by modder **Speclizer**. In development since January 2026, with a
planned September 2026 release, partly funded through Patreon (intended to be free
at release).

**Outcome**: on **2026-09-13/14** Speclizer announced that a letter on behalf of
**Sony Interactive Entertainment** had requested the mod not be released. The
project was scrapped; work-in-progress footage remains on YouTube, and the modder
pivoted to an original project.

**Context**: Naughty Dog cancelled its own standalone **The Last of Us Online** in
December 2023 at roughly 80% complete (director Vinit Agarwal); former Sony
executive Shuhei Yoshida later said he wished it had shipped. A fan mod standing
up the same feature is a direct overlap with something the publisher chose not to
ship — and still controls.

**Lesson**: "the official version doesn't exist" is not a safe harbour. The rights
holder can assert the concept, and a partially funded project has no leverage.
