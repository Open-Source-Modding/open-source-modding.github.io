'''''[[Oblivion:Oblivion Remastered|Oblivion Remastered]]''''' features extensive changes from the original release of ''[[Oblivion:Oblivion|Oblivion]]'', ranging from the game's presentation, to gameplay adjustments, to technical changes under the hood. ==New Content==
===Clairvoyance===
[[File:OBR-misc-Clairvoyance.jpg|right|thumb|Example of Clairvoyance spell in action]]
A new [[Oblivion:Clairvoyance|Clairvoyance]] spell effect has been added, guiding the player towards their current objective in a similar fashion to the same effect featured in Skyrim. A single spell utilizing this effect exists in the game, and is given to the player character at the beginning of the game.

==Bugs Fixed==
===Exploits===
* [[Oblivion:Exploitable Glitches#Duplicate Items Using Scrolls|Scroll duplication glitch]] can no longer be performed.
===Items===
* The [[Oblivion:Mace of Molag Bal|Mace of Molag Bal]]'s [[Oblivion:Absorb Strength|Absorb Strength]] enchantment now lasts 10 seconds instead of 0.
* The [[Oblivion:Base Ring of Steelskin|Base Ring of Steelskin]] now has a [[Oblivion:Resist Normal Weapons|Resist Normal Weapons]] enchantment like the other Steelskin rings.
* The leveled effects of the [[Oblivion:Mind and Body Ring|Mind and Body Ring]] are now assigned in the conventional order.
* The leveled effects of the [[Oblivion:Amulet of Interrogation|Amulet of Interrogation]] are now assigned in the conventional order.
* The [[Oblivion:Bloodworm_Helm|Bloodworm Helm]]'s [[Oblivion:Leveled_Spells#Essence_Drain|Essence Drain]] spell now correctly levels with the item level.
* [[Shivering:Nerveshatter|Nerveshatter]] now correctly applies [[Oblivion:Weakness to Shock|Weakness to Shock]] damage.
* The [[Oblivion:Necromancer's_Amulet|Necromancer's Amulet]] now scales to the player instead of [[Oblivion:Caranya|Caranya]].
* The [[Oblivion:Unique Items#Spectre Ring|Spectre Ring]] is now correctly owned and purchasable from [[Oblivion:Hamlof Red-Tooth|Hamlof Red-Tooth]].
* The [[Oblivion:Oghma_Infinium|Oghma Infinium]]'s Path of Spirit now correctly increases [[Oblivion:Willpower|Willpower]].
* [[Oblivion:Redwave|Redwave]] now has leveled damage and durability. However, its enchantment is unchanged.

===Quests===
* '''[[Oblivion:Cheydinhal Recommendation|Cheydinhal Recommendation]]''': The unique [[Oblivion:Black Soul Gem|Black Soul Gems]] encountered during the quest now have their max soul level set to None, ensuring the player cannot fill them with souls and then use them, preventing the player from completing the quest.
* '''[[Oblivion:Leyawiin Recommendation|Leyawiin Recommendation]]''': [[Oblivion:Kalthar|Kalthar]] no longer has a key when he's alive, instead, it only spawns on his body once he is dead. As the player is unable to pickpocket the key prematurely, they must defeat Kalthar in order to leave Fort Blueblood, so the quest can no longer be softlocked.
* '''[[Oblivion:Canvas the Castle|Canvas the Castle]]''': The painting of Charrus Valga will now appear in [[Oblivion:Countess Arriana Valga|Countess Arriana Valga]]'s private chamber if the quest is completed by turning in the correct culprit.
* '''[[Oblivion:Bittneld and Emfrid|Bittneld and Emfrid]]''': The quest now functions as intended instead of skipping directly to the conclusion.
* '''[[Oblivion:Namira|Namira]]''': The quest now correctly awards a [[Oblivion:Fame|Fame]] point upon successful completion.
* '''[[Oblivion:Affairs_of_a_Wizard|Affairs of a Wizard]]''': The quest now correctly awards an [[Oblivion:Infamy|Infamy]] point.
* [[Oblivion:Alawen|Alawen]], [[Oblivion:Bralsa Andaren|Bralsa Andaren]], [[Oblivion:Tooth-in-the-Sea|Tooth-in-the-Sea]] and [[Oblivion:Torbern|Torbern]] (master trainers and NPCs needed for quests for [[Oblivion:Marksman Training|Marksman]], [[Oblivion:Destruction Training|Destruction]], [[Oblivion:Alteration Training|Alteration]] and [[Oblivion:Acrobatics Training|Acrobatics]] training, respectively) are all set essential from the start of the game, to ensure that the related quests can no longer be started when the characters are already dead. Notably, there's a check in the script to verify their quests haven't been completed before that is done, however, as no script exists to remove their essential status afterward, they remain essential for the rest of the game.

* [[Oblivion:Paint Brush|Paint Brush]]es are now affected by gravity.
* The [[Oblivion:Fighter's Stronghold|Fighter's Stronghold]] [[Oblivion:Downloads|download]] no longer causes the [[Oblivion:Mystic Emporium|Mystic Emporium]] to become locked during the daytime.
* Learning a spell from a [[Oblivion:Spell Tomes|spell tome]] no longer plays the sound of casting that spell.

This prevents an issue where immediately selling the upgrade papers after having bought them from the merchant (without ever exiting the conversation) prevented the new upgrades from ever appearing in the house without the ability to purchase them back, as no-cost items given to the merchant do not reappear in the merchant's sales list.
* Black Rock Chest in [[Oblivion:Black Rock Caverns|Black Rock Caverns]] now correctly holds 1,766 gold in addition to the random leveled amount.
* [[Oblivion:Open|Open]] spells Fenrik's Welcome and The Unwelcome Guest, now have magnitudes of 80 and 20 as opposed to 50 and 12, respectively. This ensures they open any lock with the same label in the game, as opposed to only some.
* [[Oblivion:Wayshrines|Wayshrines]] and Nine Divines Altar blessings, as well as spells used to give the bonuses of Blood Fountains and [[Oblivion:Ayleid Wells|Ayleid Wells]] are now flagged immune to [[Oblivion:Spell Absorption|Spell Absorption]], ensuring the player cannot restore mana for free by absorbing the positive spells.
* [[Oblivion:Note from First Mate Filch|Note from First Mate Filch]] now can correctly be picked back up and taken into inventory after thrown out.
* Guards in the Green Emperor Way district of the Imperial City can now be talked to about the "Imperial Palace" topic.
* The Bandits and Marauders in Fort Nikel have a longer "aggro" range and will engage in combat with each other. However, their pathing takes them upstairs and through the area that the player will enter. They will get stuck on the gates until the player opens them. If the player is spotted, they then become the new target rather than the opposing faction.
* [[Oblivion:Dremora|Dremora]] that did not leave a [[OB:Daedra Heart|Daedra Heart]] on their corpse now correctly do so.

===Character Creation===
* References to gender have been removed from [[Oblivion:Character Creation|Character Creation]], with Female and Male body types having been renamed to "Type 1" and "Type 2", respectively. 
** The stats that were formerly determined by gender are now assigned through choosing a location [[Oblivion:Races#Oblivion Remastered|Origin]] for a character, the options of which differ for each race. The left-hand option reflects the male stats in classic ''Oblivion'', while the right-hand option reflects the female stats.
** In-game dialogue still uses gendered pronouns and forms of address (sire/ma'am, brother/sister) depending on the selected body type, as in classic ''Oblivion''. 
* Characters can now have facial hair, with a wide variety of styles available. Additionally, many NPCs in the game now sport facial hair.
* Many new eye, hair, and skin/makeup/hair tone options have been added. All eye options are now available to all races.
* Eye and hair options no longer have names, with the player selecting an icon from the list instead. 
* Every NPC face in the game has been redesigned. Some remain visually faithful to their original appearance, while others have been radically changed, no longer resembling their look in classic ''Oblivion''.
* The character limit on names has been increased to 30 characters.

===General===
* [[Oblivion:Achievements|Achievements]] are available for PC and PlayStation players to earn for the first time. Using [[Oblivion:Console|console commands]] will disable the ability to earn achievements, and affected save files are indicated with a broken trophy icon.
** Achievement Xbox Gamerscore values have been adjusted down, bringing the total from 1250 across Oblivion and Shivering Isles, to just 1000.
** Grey Fox achievement has been renamed Guildmaster.
** A new achievement has been added solely for Playstation: Worldly God, which requires getting all other achievements.
* You can now move slowly while over-encumbered instead of being frozen in place — similar to ''Skyrim'', except at a slower pace than standard walking. As in ''Skyrim'', you are unable to run, sprint, or jump while over-encumbered. You also cannot dodge, power attack, shield bash, or fast travel.
* All downloadable content from classic ''Oblivion'' is enabled by default, with no ability to disable them outside of modding. As a consequence, some content only present without the DLCs is inaccessible.
